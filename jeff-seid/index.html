Psychology and mechanics of the launch

1

Preframe

Fri 7 to Sun 16 Aug

Get the audience accustomed to Jeff openly talking about brand deals and making money with his audience. Establish credibility, frame as he's been getting a ton of questions on these topics from creators and want to be creators.

Rules No date, no product, nothing that hints a launch is coming. Two story frames a day minimum, and in week one the cadence matters more than what he actually says.

Fri 7 Aug

Start talking about money, and find out who we are actually talking to. Two polls to size the audience, plus the first proof reel.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
  • Comments
Sat 8 Aug

Make it obvious people are asking him this. He is not bringing it up to sell something.

  • Stories x2
Sun 9 Aug

Show he started where they are. An early deal, and what he got wrong on it.

  • Stories x2
Mon 10 AugGiveaway drops

Get contact details off the people paying attention. Release the first brand deal training on the keyword, and open the Telegram channel.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
  • Opt-in form
  • Telegram opens
Tue 11 Aug

Keep people claiming the training now the announcement is a day old.

  • Stories x2
  • DMs
  • Telegram
Wed 12 Aug

Get them to see how badly they are being paid. Collect what brands are offering them, and react to it out loud.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
Thu 13 Aug

Reach the people who would actually buy but never open stories.

  • Stories x2
  • YouTube
Fri 14 Aug

Kill the idea that followers are what gets you paid.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
Sat 15 Aug

Keep it looking like he is answering questions, not building up to something.

  • Stories x2
Sun 16 AugPreframe ends

Put the strongest proof out the day before the date drops. What he charged then against what he charges now.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
2

Whisper

Mon 17 to Thu 20 Aug

Get one date locked in their head and nothing else. Make them want to know what it is without telling them.

Rules No product named, no links anywhere. Two frames a day, poll first then the message, 30 August in every single frame.

Mon 17 AugWhisper starts

Get 30 August in front of everyone, everywhere, on the same day.

  • Stories x2
  • Email
  • Telegram
  • Pages up, unlinked
  • VSL + thank you video
Tue 18 Aug

Show he turns work down, which is what someone in demand does.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
  • Email
Wed 19 Aug

Make them want to know what it is. Hint at what he's covering without naming it.

  • Stories x2
  • Email
Thu 20 AugWhisper ends

Last date drop before it gets a name.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
  • YouTube
  • Email
3

Tease

Fri 21 to Mon 24 Aug

Name it and open registration. Get as many of the right people signed up as possible before the free asset takes over.

Rules Registration is the only ask for four days. Three heavy frames spaced two days apart. Keyword first and link second.

Fri 21 AugRegistration opens

Open registration and get as many people signed up on day one as possible. Name it, say how many seats, put the link everywhere.

  • Heavy frame
  • Story
  • Announcement reel
  • Pages live
  • Telegram
  • Email x2-4
  • DMs
Sat 22 Aug

Keep registrations coming without pitching hard two days running. Post the count so far.

  • Stories x2
  • Email x2-4
  • DMs
Sun 23 AugSMS opens

Get the wrong people to opt out on their own, so the room is people who could actually buy.

  • Heavy frame
  • Story
  • Reel
  • Email x2-4
  • SMS every 2 days
Mon 24 AugTease ends

Use seats left to make people decide now, before the free asset takes over the feed.

  • Heavy frame
  • Story
  • Email x2-4
  • Asset keyword live
4

Free Asset

Tue 25 to Thu 27 Aug

Bring new people in. Three weeks in, the same audience has seen everything, so a real free asset pulls in people who have not been watching.

Rules Registration stays the ask underneath it. New registrants pick up the emails at the phase they joined, not back at the start.

Tue 25 AugAsset drops

Bring new people in. Release the pricing asset on the keyword and straight into the channel.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
  • Email
  • Telegram
  • SMS daily
Wed 26 Aug

Keep the asset in front of people who haven't claimed it.

  • Stories x2
  • Email
  • SMS
Thu 27 Aug

Last day before it gets loud. Tell them what actually happens on the 30th.

  • Stories x2
  • Reel
  • YouTube
  • Email
  • SMS
5

Push

Fri 28 to Sat 29 Aug

The loudest two days of the launch. Most people register in the last 48 hours, and anyone who signs up now still gets every reminder before the webinar.

Rules Volume goes up here, not down. No going quiet before the room.

Fri 28 AugCountdown starts

Start the countdown and give them a reason to stay to the end. The giveaway rule goes out.

  • Stories x3
  • Countdown reel
  • Email peak
  • Telegram
  • SMS daily
  • DMs
  • Pre-call video live
Sat 29 Aug24hr reminder

Get everything the last 24 hours will give. Anyone still not registered called out by name.

  • Stories x3
  • Last call reel
  • 24hr reminder
  • Email peak
  • Telegram
  • SMS daily
  • DMs
6

Webinar Day

Sun 30 Aug

Fill the room, hold them to the end, and book calls while they still believe it.

Sun 30 AugWEBINAR

Get them in, keep them in, and book them before the feeling wears off.

  • Reminder ladder x6
  • Telegram at every rung
  • Stories all day
  • Live today reel
  • Live 45-60 min
  • Sign-up demo
  • Giveaway drawn
  • Buyer welcome video
  • Follow up same night
7

Close

Mon 31 Aug to Sun 6 Sep

Work the replay and the booked list for a full working week. That's what running on a Sunday buys.

Mon 31 AugReplay live

Catch everyone who missed it and get the calls moving.

  • Replay public
  • Telegram
  • No-show sequence
  • No-show recap video
  • Retargeting
  • Calls from 9am
Tue 1 to Sat 5 Sep

Work every list daily until the cart shuts. Nothing new gets built, everything already running gets run.

  • Replay sequence
  • Retargeting
  • Calls daily
  • Email daily
Sun 6 SepCART CLOSES

Last call. Take everything the deadline will give.

  • Final emails
  • Telegram last call
  • Offer closes

Wed 9 Sep

Every buyer onboarded, inside 72 hours of buying.

  • Onboarding

Partnerships assigned, and tracking live before anyone posts.
